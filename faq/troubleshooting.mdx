Most issues = missing permission, misconfigured setting, or role hierarchy.

## No Response to Commands

1. Wraith online?
2. **Read Messages** \+ **Send Messages** in the channel (channel deny overrides server allow)
3. Correct prefix? `@Wraith prefix`
4. Try `@Wraith help`

## Can't Assign a Role

Hierarchy issue. **Server Settings → Roles** — drag Wraith above the target role.

<Warning>
  Wraith must sit above **every** role it manages (jail, mute, level rewards, etc.).
</Warning>

## Welcome / Goodbye Not Sending

1. `,welcome channel` to verify it's set
2. Wraith needs **Send Messages** in that channel
3. `,welcome test`

## Antinuke Not Triggering

1. `,antinuke config` — verify it's on and the module is enabled
2. Wraith's role at the **top** of hierarchy
3. Check whitelist for unintended entries

Single actions don't trigger antinuke — it's threshold-based.

## Logs Missing

1. `,logs` to verify channel
2. Needs **Send Messages**, **Embed Links**, **Manage Webhooks**
3. Message-delete attribution needs server-level **View Audit Log**

## VoiceMaster Not Creating

1. Did you run `,voicemaster setup`?
2. **Manage Channels** on the category
3. Trigger must be a **voice** channel

## Music Not Working

1. Join a voice channel first
2. Wraith needs **Connect** \+ **Speak**
3. Channel user limit not full

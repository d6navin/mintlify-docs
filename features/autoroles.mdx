Auto-assign roles on join. Separate config for humans and bots.

## Humans

```text
,autorole add @Role
,autorole remove @Role
,autorole list
```

## Bots

```text
,autorole bot add @BotRole
,autorole bot remove @BotRole
```

Stacks on top of any pre-selected invite roles.

---

<Warning>
  Wraith's role must sit **above** every autorole. Otherwise Discord blocks assignment.
</Warning>

<Accordion title="Members who join while Wraith is offline">
  Won't receive autoroles — Discord doesn't replay join events. Assign manually.
</Accordion>
